What Is Computer Vision

Uses of Computer Vision

Healthcare

Computer vision is used in the medical industry to automate and improve the accuracy of medial imaging. It is often used to find things such as

  • Fractures
  • Tumors
  • Cardiovascular issues

Agriculture

The Agriculture industry makes use of Computer Vision in many ways.

  • Monitor Crop Health
  • Track Livestock
  • Detect Plant Disease

Document Processing

A boring but necessary function. Computer Vision is the backbone for document digitization and document OCR. Converting paper documents into digital text.
