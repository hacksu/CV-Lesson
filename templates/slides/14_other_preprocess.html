Other Types of Pre-Processing

Gaussian Blur / Denoising

Images with lots of noise can be slightly blurred to remove that noise.

Normalization

Instead of storing pixels on a scale of 0-255, we can normalize to a floating point number between -1 and 1. Helps models converge quicker.

Letter Boxing

Another step of image resizing. Adds a neutral color that is not later processed. Pads the smaller dimension out till image is a perfect square for better processing.
