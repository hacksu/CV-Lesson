Resizing

Many machine learning models used in Computer Vision are also trained off of a specific size of image. Processing differently sized images can cause a loss of quality or a unwanted clipping of the images content. Many models clip to some square image size such as 256x256 or 640x640.

Smaller images can also be much quicker to process. If perfect quality is not needed reducing the amount of data will speed up processing. a 500*500 image is 250 thousand pixels to process while a 2000*2000 is 4 million.

Images that are not the correct size have to be either clipped, expanded, or resized into the correct format. There are many ways to do this but some of the most common include interpolation, and stretching.
