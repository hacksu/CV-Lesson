Issues with Classical Solutions

Classic computer vision processing methods were revolutionary and had many years of fine-tuning. Processes such as HOG + SVM allowed for relatively complex detection of things like Cars and Pedestrians, but required careful finetuning. Many of these classical processes had to be finetuned by hand to work on a small subset of objects, and would break with new data.
