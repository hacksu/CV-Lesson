Other Types of Classical Detection

While edge detection is one of the most commonly used types of classical computer vision processing, other models do exist that serve different purposes.

Threshold Detection

Grayscale mask over entire image, convert values below threshold to black and above to white. Useful for pulling subjects out or removing backgrounds for OCR.

Blob Detection

Early form of object recognition that uses significant differences in pixel values to split images into blobs of similar objects.
