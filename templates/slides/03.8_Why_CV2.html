What Is Computer Vision

Uses of Computer Vision

To Win Hackathons

Judges love computer vision projects, and they usually are not that complicated to set up once you know what tools you have at your disposal.

'Surveillance'

As we will see later, computer vision can be used to track where objects, including people, are in a room! This has been the backbone of many of our most beloved companies. We can track things such as

  • Cars
  • Clothing Items
  • People

'Defense'

Computer Vision's ability to track things also makes it vital in the defense industry, powering things like

  • Airplane Flying
  • Unmanned Vehicles
  • Anti-Missile Systems
  • Missile Systems
