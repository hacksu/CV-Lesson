Modern CV Tools

YOLO (You Only Look Once)

YOLO is a very popular family of object detection models provided by Ultralytics.

  • Process Images in a single pass, splits the image into regions, and each region predicts boxes, labels, and confidence scores
  • Filters out duplicate detections
  • Useful for real time object detection and tracking

Google's MediaPipe

Google's MediaPipe is another extremely popular family of models that handle different types of body tracking.

  • Differnt models for hands, face, or body landmarks
  • 2 step system where a body is first detected and then cropped around for landmark detection
  • Outputs landmark coordinates for body tracking
